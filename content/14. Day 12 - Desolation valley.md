The **Doros Crater** is a large, remote volcanic feature in north-western Namibia, often described as a collapsed volcanic caldera rather than an impact crater, measuring roughly 3–4 km in diameter and rising subtly above the surrounding desert plains; its origin is linked to ancient volcanic activity associated with the Etendeka flood basalts, and today it presents as a broad ring of low hills enclosing a sparsely vegetated interior. Reached only by challenging 4×4 tracks and requiring careful navigation, the Doros Crater is valued less for dramatic relief than for its geological significance, profound isolation, and the sense of deep time it conveys—making it a niche but rewarding destination for experienced Kaokoveld and Damaraland travellers seeking true wilderness rather than conventional landmarks.

### Divorce pass

"Divorce Pass" in Namibia is ==a notoriously challenging 4x4 track in the rugged Damaraland region==, known for its steep, rocky terrain, rock slides, and sharp climbs/descents, requiring high clearance and careful driving, rewarding adventurers with stunning scenery and a significant sense of achievement. It's a popular, difficult adventure route for overlanders connecting areas like Twyfelfontein towards the Ugab River, often taking hours to traverse. 

**Key Characteristics of Divorce Pass:**

- **Location:** Damaraland, Erongo Region, near Brandberg Mountain and Ugab River.
- **Difficulty:** Very rocky, steep, requires high clearance and a 4x4 vehicle; not for the faint-hearted.
- **Experience:** Exciting, adventurous, with opportunities for photography and exploring remote landscapes.
- **Duration:** Can take a significant portion of a day to complete safely. 

![[Pasted image 20260118091202.png]]

**Why it's Called Divorce Pass:**

- The name reflects the pass's demanding nature, suggesting it's a trial that tests relationships and vehicles, similar to the challenges of a divorce itself. 

**What to Expect if Driving It:**

- **Vehicle:** A proper 4x4 with high clearance is essential.
- **Preparation:** Lower tire pressure for better grip, take your time, and be prepared for slow, careful driving.
- **Rewards:** Breathtakingly beautiful views and an unforgettable adventure.


>[!Quote] We drove this pass on April 25th after heavy rain, which was already difficult, and had to turn back because of the subsequent blockage by the rather high river... it was quite nerve-wracking, although much easier than the van Zyls Pass. (April 2025)

>[!Quote]  This is a stunning pass but again it must not be under estimated. It is technically difficult and should not be taken lightly. Steep inclines Sharp jagged rocks near cliff passes and can get extremely hot. take your time. and use caution. it will take you the better part of 3 hours to complete so be aware you have sufficient time to get in and out. The views are stunning and once completed it is a significant achievement. (March 2024)

>[!Quote] Definitely set aside a full day for this route. We drove from north to south, and the last 6 km alone took over 1.5 hours. It's essential to have someone go ahead on difficult sections. We had to fill in some deep potholes with stones to drive over them. Otherwise, it's a fantastic route with breathtaking scenery. We saw fresh elephant tracks everywhere in Goantagab. A little tip: You don't necessarily have to drive over Divorca Pass to get to Brandberg Mine. You can also stay in Goantagab from Doros Crater to reach the mine. (January 2021)
