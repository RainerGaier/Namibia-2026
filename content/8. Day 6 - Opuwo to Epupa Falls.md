### Camp vibes

>
>Umarunga campsite - There are 10 campsites, each with a fire pit, a water tap and a light. 
>A thatched ablution facility is available, as well as a scullery and a laundry basin. 
>Plug points are limited and only available at certain times of the day.
>


![[Pasted image 20260110051106.png|600]]

>
>The main lodge offers a public bar and restaurant available to guests, as well as an outdoor communal swimming pool for those hot summer days. 
>Various excursions can be booked at the campsite for an additional charge.
