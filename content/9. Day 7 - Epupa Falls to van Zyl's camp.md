>
>This where the exploration really gets interesting - where the feeling of the wilderness takes root, from a landscape, remoteness and riding perspective.
>
>Only 145kms but will take several hours going through this very unique countryside. 
>
>The first 80kms is easy going and also the last easy going section for many kms.
>
>The next 65kms is beautiful riding across jeep tracks heading toward the infamous van Zyl's pass. 
>
>
>The D3703 is known as the “Tyre Eater Pass”. 
>
>Many roads (tracks) in Kaokaland have broken up rocks with very sharp edges, deposited by the South African Defence Force so that their vehicles could supply the Guard Posts along the Cunene. 
>
>From Van Zyl’s up through the Marienfluss, is another danger area where the long grass gets caught up in the exhaust pipes and catches alight especially in petrol driven vehicles which run much hotter than diesel.
>
