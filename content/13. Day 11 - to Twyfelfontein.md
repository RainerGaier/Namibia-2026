### Overnight Stay - Desert Elephant Campsite


>
>We'll be camping at Desert Elephant - about 100kms from Palmwag.
>
>A very basic camping site for our last night.
>
>Each campsite is shaded and has its own fireplace. 
>
>All campsites have access to a communal kitchen facility and shared  ablution with showers and flush toilets. 
>
>Each campsite accommodates a maximum of eight to twelve people.
>
>No electricity. 
>
