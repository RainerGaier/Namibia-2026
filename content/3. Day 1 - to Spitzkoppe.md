
### Up the Umaruru river

>
>We will confirm the conditions of the riverbeds prior to doing this and other sections of the route, but assuming conditions are good, we'll travel about 80kms inside the Umaruru riverbed, hang right and head down to Spitzkoppe. 
>
>There is a good chance the support vehicles can follow on the same route. If not, there is an alternate route available, but super corrugated, I'm told.
>
>
>We should leave at first light to give us as much time as possible to enjoy the route and to get to Spitzkoppe early as possible - to enjoy this magical place.

### Spitzkoppe

> 
> The Spitzkoppe is a group of bald granite peaks or inselbergs. The sheer size with voluptuous curves makes this cluster formation so intriguing. The granite is more than 120 million years old and the highest outcrop rises about 1,728 metres above sea level. The peaks stand out dramatically from the flat surrounding plains.
>
