### Visas

Visa requirements for Namibia depend on your nationality, but all travellers need ==a passport valid for at least six months beyond their stay and with at least three blank pages==. Some nationalities can get a visa on arrival, while others must apply online beforehand or through an embassy. All travellers should also have a valid return ticket. 

*Key requirements for all visitors*

- **Passport:** Must be valid for at least six months after your departure date and have at least three blank pages. 
- **Return ticket:** You must have a valid return or onward ticket. 
- **Yellow fever vaccination:** A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required if arriving from a country with a risk of yellow fever. 

*Visa on arrival or advance application*

- **Online application:** 
    To avoid delays, it is recommended to apply for an e-Visa online before your trip. An online application can be submitted through the <a href="https://eservices.mhaiss.gov.na/visaonarrival" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MHAISS E-Services Portal</a>
- **Visa on arrival:** 
    Some nationalities can get a visa upon arrival at major entry points, but it is still recommended to apply online beforehand. 
- **Application details:** 
    You will need a completed application form, a valid passport, and confirmation of accommodation. 

*Visa exemptions*

- Citizens from certain countries, such as Botswana, Kenya, South Africa, and Zimbabwe, do not require a visa for a stay of up to 90 days for tourism, visiting friends/relatives, or business. 
- Some countries, including the USA and UK, now require a visa on arrival or an advance e-Visa application as of April 1, 2025.
